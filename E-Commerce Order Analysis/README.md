# 📦 MR’DYWS E-Commerce Order Analysis (Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **MR’DYWS e-commerce order data** to evaluate sales performance, customer payment behavior, delivery efficiency, and product demand across states and product categories.

Using **Microsoft Excel**, the dashboard provides a centralized view of operational and revenue metrics to support business decision-making, inventory planning, and payment strategy optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The key objectives of this analysis are to:
- Analyze total sales performance and order volume
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Identify top-selling products and categories
- Evaluate delivery efficiency
- Track revenue trends across time and states

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
  - Slicers for Year filtering
  - KPI cards
  - Data aggregation and trend analysis
  - Dashboard design and layout

## 🌍 Geographic Sales Analysis
- **Top Performing States by Sales:**
  - Oyo
  - Rivers
  - Abuja
  - Lagos

Sales distribution indicates strong regional demand concentration, suggesting opportunities for targeted marketing and logistics optimization.

---

## 📆 Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue peaked in **March**
- Stable performance observed from **January to June**
- Significant decline recorded in **December**, indicating possible seasonality or data gaps

---

## ⭐ Product Ratings Analysis
- Products with higher sales volumes generally maintain **strong customer ratings**
- Jerseys and Sneakers show both **high demand and favorable ratings**
- Lower-rated products may require quality review or pricing adjustments

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Payment methods are evenly distributed, showing **no dominant customer preference**
- Fast average delivery time (3 days) supports customer satisfaction
- Clothing and household products drive the majority of sales
- Regional demand varies significantly across states

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Increase inventory for high-demand products such as Jerseys and Gas Cylinders
- Leverage strong delivery performance in marketing campaigns
- Investigate December revenue decline to address potential operational issues
- Use regional sales insights to optimize distribution and promotions
- Monitor lower-rated products to improve quality or customer experience
