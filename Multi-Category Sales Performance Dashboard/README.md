# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (2023–2024) – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales data across multiple business dimensions, including time, product categories, customer demographics, and regions.

The dashboard compares sales performance for **2023 and 2024**, providing insights into revenue growth, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The objectives of this dashboard are to:
- Monitor overall sales performance using key metrics
- Analyze year-on-year revenue growth
- Identify underperforming products that need attention
- Understand customer contribution by gender and age segment
- Evaluate regional sales performance
- Compare weekday and weekend sales patterns

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Pivot Tables
  - Pivot Charts
  - Slicers and Filters
  - Calculated fields
- **Excel Data Cleaning & Transformation**

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset consists of transactional sales records containing:
- Sales revenue
- Units sold
- Product category and product name
- Customer details (gender and age group)
- Sales date
- Region

The data covers **two years (2023 and 2024)** across the following categories:
- Electronics  
- Groceries  
- Clothing  
- Home Appliances  
- Sports  

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Units Sold:** 756  
- **Overall Sales Revenue:** $398,547  
- **Average Revenue per Customer:** $6,755  
- **Average Revenue per Product:** $39,855  

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of business performance.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Year-on-Year Revenue Analysis
- Revenue increased by **36.33%** from 2023 to 2024
- 2024 outperformed 2023 across most months
- Strong sales performance observed towards the end of the year

### Monthly Revenue Trend
- Sales show noticeable seasonality
- Revenue peaks in **November and December**
- Mid-year months show moderate performance

### Time-Based Sales Analysis
- **79%** of total revenue is generated on weekdays
- **21%** of revenue comes from weekends
- Monday, Friday, and Saturday record the highest sales

### Product Performance
- Several products are identified as underperforming and require strategic attention
- Sports and Groceries generate the highest revenue by category
- Clothing and Home Appliances show growth potential

### Customer Analysis
- Female customers contribute **78%** of total revenue
- Male customers contribute **22%**
- Middle-aged and older customers generate higher revenue
- Top 5 customers contribute a significant share of total sales

### Regional Performance
- North and West regions record the highest revenue
- South region shows moderate performance
- East region has the lowest revenue and growth opportunity
