# 📊 E-Commerce Order Analysis (Excel Project)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end analysis of **MR’DYWS E-Commerce Orders** using **Microsoft Excel**.  
The analysis focuses on evaluating sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, delivery efficiency, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Pivot Tables
  - Pivot Charts
  - Slicers
  - Calculated Fields
  - Excel Functions (SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, Percentage Calculations)

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains transactional e-commerce data with the following key fields:
- Order ID
- Order Date
- Product Name
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Quantity Sold
- Sales Amount
- Customer State
- Payment Method
- Delivery Time (Days)
- Product Ratings

---

## 🎯 Objectives of the Analysis
The objectives of this analysis are to:
- Evaluate overall sales and order performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze customer payment preferences
- Examine sales distribution across states
- Track monthly revenue trends
- Assess delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:
- **Total Orders:** 1,111
- **Total Sales:** ₦118.32M
- **Average Sales per Order:** ₦106,503
- **Average Delivery Time:** 3 Days

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 🔹 Sales Performance
- Total sales generated amounted to **₦118.32M**, indicating strong overall performance.
- Accessories, Kitchen, and Clothing sub-categories recorded the highest sales, showing strong customer demand in these areas.

### 🔹 Top Products by Quantity Sold
- The top-selling products by quantity include:
  - Jersey
  - Gas Cylinder
  - Sneakers
  - Electric Kettle
  - Power Bank
- These products contribute significantly to total sales volume and should be prioritized for inventory planning.

### 🔹 Sales by State
- Sales are highest in **Oyo, Rivers, Abuja, and Lagos**, indicating strong regional demand.
- These states represent key markets for targeted marketing and logistics optimization.

### 🔹 Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue shows noticeable fluctuations across months.
- Peak sales are observed in the first half of the year, while a sharp decline is recorded in December.
- This suggests possible seasonality, inventory shortages, or reduced marketing activities during that period.

### 🔹 Payment Method Analysis
- Orders are almost evenly distributed across payment methods:
  - **Card Payments:** 33.73%
  - **Cash on Delivery:** 32.88%
  - **Bank Transfers:** 33.39%
- This indicates diverse customer payment preferences, making it important to maintain multiple payment options.

### 🔹 Product Ratings
- Most products received higher ratings (3-star and above), indicating satisfactory customer experience.
- Products with consistently lower ratings may require quality improvement or better customer support.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High-performing product categories drive the majority of revenue.
- Certain states contribute disproportionately to total sales.
- Delivery time remains efficient with an average of 3 days.
- Payment behavior is well-balanced, reducing dependency on a single payment method.

---

## ✅ Recommendations
- **Inventory Optimization:** Increase stock levels for top-selling products such as Jerseys and Gas Cylinders.
- **Regional Marketing:** Focus advertising efforts on high-performing states like Lagos, Abuja, Rivers, and Oyo.
- **Seasonality Strategy:** Investigate the sharp revenue drop in December and implement promotional campaigns to boost sales.
- **Customer Experience:** Improve products with lower ratings through quality checks and customer feedback analysis.
- **Payment Flexibility:** Continue supporting multiple payment methods to accommodate diverse customer preferences.

---

## 📌 Conclusion
This Excel-based analysis provides valuable insights into sales trends, customer behavior, and operational efficiency.  
The findings can help stakeholders improve decision-making in inventory management, marketing strategy, and customer experience, ultimately driving business growth.
